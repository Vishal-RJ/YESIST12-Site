ABOUT IENGAGE

IEngage Track (Industry Engagement) bridges students, professionals and future entrepreneurs with the industry by making them ready for the future. Sharpen your skills, unlock your potential, and witness innovation come alive. This track is all set to take you to the next level of product and startup mastery by providing training on foundational aspects from the Basics level of Cutting-Edge Technologies.

IEngage Track has a key focus on Career Development, Industrial Skills Development and Startup/Entrepreneurship Development with our idea âNew Age IEngageâ

Vision:

By partnering with industry experts, YESIST12 unlocks exclusive career development opportunities, in-demand skill development workshops, and entrepreneurial mentorship for participants, empowering them to thrive in their chosen fields.

Mission:

  1. Creating pathways for pre-university and university students to collaborate with industry professionals on projects that address critical social challenges.
  2. Unleashing the power of social responsibility through community engagement.
  3. We empower young minds to become tech-powered changemakers for a brighter tomorrow.

Pillars

Career Building

We empower young professionals to build a strong foundation for their careers by equipping them with in-demand technical skills and allow them to demonstrate with innovations.

Industry Skills Development

Embrace continuous learning and develop industry knowledge to stay relevant and competitive.

Start-up/ Entrepreneurship Development

IEngage provides right guidance with the three stages of startup namely early-stage, venture-funded (growth) stage and late stage. IEngage unlocks your entrepreneurial potential and fuels your confidence for success.

Newage IEngage

This dynamic platform is designed to empower you to:

Learn: Immerse yourself in engaging events, workshops, and sessions led by industry experts, covering a wide range of topics like technology, entrepreneurship, marketing, and more.

Network: Connect with like-minded individuals and industry professionals to share ideas, build valuable relationships, and spark collaborations.

Develop: Hone your skills and knowledge through interactive sessions and hands-on projects, gaining practical experience and valuable insights into the latest trends and best practices.

Collaborate: Engage in meaningful discussions and create a collaborative environment, fostering innovation and learning from diverse perspectives.

Grow: Develop a project under the guidance of industry experts, turning your ideas into reality and taking your career to the next level.

Mentor Mentee Program:

Mentor-Mentee Program is a structured learning and development program that pairs experienced individuals (mentors) with less experienced individuals (mentees) for guidance, support, and knowledge transfer. We will be finding out the right mentors who can take YESIST12 Participants as their Mentees and guide them through the solution building journey. The best mentor-mentee pair will be identified and rewarded.

FAQs | IEngage Track

1. What does IEngage Track focus on?

We focus on taking innovators and participants to the next level of product and startup building.

2. What benefits will the participants be getting from IEngage Track?

They get the right mentorship and training from industry experts to improve their startup and professional journey

3. How IEngage Track different from other tracks of IEEE YESIST12? Or Why IEngage Track?

IEngage Track differs from the other tracks by providing the winners of the other tracks and participants of YESIST12 with right knowledge on making the product dream as true.

4. Will there be any Call for Abstracts for IEngage Track?

There is no call for abstracts for IEngage Track as IEngage track is known for engaging the participants of YESISt12 with proper path to Entrepreneurial and Professional success

6. Who can attend IEngage Track events?

All those who are interested to take their project ideas, abstracts to the final product and become a successful entrepreneur and those who want to grow ahead in their professional journey.

7. What topics will be covered in the IEngage Track sessions?

IEngage Sessions will cover methodologies and guidance to Career Development, Industrial Skills Development and Startup/Entrepreneurship Development.

8. How do I register for the IEngage Track sessions?

IEngage Track sessions will be open for registration via Google Form or Website Links.

9. Will there be any competitions or presentations in the IEngage Track?

The IEngage Track will not include any formal competitions or presentations. However, there will be opportunities for participants to network, share their ideas, and receive feedback from experts in the field.

10. Will there be any prizes or awards for participating in the IEngage Track sessions?

No prizes or awards for participating in the IEngage Track Sessions but you get valuable information from keynote speakers and industry experts.

11. What is the Mentor-Mentee Program?

It is a dedicated program to connect industry professionals with YESIST12 Participants and mentor them to provide the best solutions for the problems they are working on.
