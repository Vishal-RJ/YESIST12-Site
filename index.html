ABOUT YESIST12

IEEE YESIST 12 (YOUTH ENDEAVORS FOR SOCIAL INNOVATION USING SUSTAINABLE TECHNOLOGY)

is an revolutionary initiative that unifies social responsibility and ethics in young minds and unleash the power of fresh ideas that have the potential to reshape communities through captivating activities. It provides a platform for students and young professionals to showcase their innovative ideas to find the key to humanitarian and social issues affecting directly the community around them. The talent show seeks to encourage service-oriented engineering practice and inspire the next generation of engineers to embrace social responsibility and ethical practices. It's more than a showcase; it's a call to action, promoting a community of service-oriented engineering practitioners committed to making a real difference.

Why should you join YESIST12?

Global Perspectives

YESIST12 is a melting pot of creativity, each contributing a unique approach to solving critical challenges and amplify their impact through shared knowledge, resources, and support networks.

Innovative Solutions for Impact

Dig through innovative solutions from students and young professionals to empower change makers, tackle global challenges and examine a diverse array of innovative solutions crafted by passionate minds.

Forum for Collaboration

Connect with professionals, innovators, and enthusiasts worldwide. Co-create with fellow visionaries, fostering collaboration and networking chances. It’s a dynamic hub where partnership thrives.

Expert Mentorship

Get in benefits from tailored expert guidance and mentorship to refine your ideas. Our mentors bring strategic insight that can shape your critical decisions, turn creative ideas into impactful solutions and chart courses towards success.

Drive in Entrepreneurship

Ideas have the power to change entities, Entrepreneurs will provide valuable insights to uplift your entrepreneurial notions and navigate the path to impactful ventures and the dynamic landscape of entrepreneurship.

Empower Through Technology

YESIST12 fuels innovation and creativity, entrusting individuals to unleash their creativity and bring their ideas to the realm world along with the opportunity to explore the intersection of technology and social change.

Our Tracks

Innovation Challenge

Innovation Challenge is an international project competition established by IEEE Madras Section and IEEE Education Society to encourage the participants to come up with innovative ideas for the social and humanitarian issues.

Know More

Junior Einstein

Junior Einstein Track acts as a catalyst for innovation and provides a transformative opportunity to broaden the horizon of knowledge among the young curious minds.

Maker Fair

MakerFair is a unique platform with opportunities for the innovators, makers, and creators to showcase their marvelous products to the international community.
Know More
Know More

WePOWER

The World Bank recently started SAGE initiative with a plan to establish a WePOWER to promote women engineers and practitioners in the energy and power sector, and bring normative change regarding women in STEM education.

Special Track

Special Track aims at bringing out the innovative solutions to solve issues of mankind, on a global scale. It is the recently added track in YESIST12 during its 2020 edition and focused on health and disaster management.

IEngage Track

IEngage Track (Industry Engagement) is an unique track with the keen focus to bridge the gap between Industry and Students’ community. It is all set to take innovators of YESIST12 to the next level of product & startup building stage.
